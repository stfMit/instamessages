How to Use on Your Devices

  1. Native Desktop App: Run npm start in this project folder for the dedicated standalone window with CSS injection active.
  2. Browser / PWA: In Chrome or Edge, click the Install icon in your URL address bar to install it as an app on your desktop taskbar.
  3. Browser Extension: Load the included extension/ folder into Chrome/Edge for 100% distraction-free web browsing.
  1. Open this PWA in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the Share button (box with an arrow pointing up) at the bottom.
  3. Scroll down and select "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Tap Add in the top-right. You now have an InstaDM icon on your home screen!
  1. Open this PWA in Chrome on your Android phone.
  2. Tap the three dots (⋮) menu in the top right.
  3. Select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  4. Confirm install. The app opens directly to your inbox from your launcher.
